Better Claude RC

Drive Claude Code on your Mac — from your phone.

Kick off sessions, review diffs, approve permissions and drop into a real terminal from anywhere. End-to-end encrypted: the relay moves ciphertext and can't read a byte of your code.

No password — a single-use magic link signs you in. Already signed in on this phone? Open the app.

How it works

  1. Sign in here and add your Mac from the dashboard.
  2. Enroll the agent — paste the one-line command from your dashboard into a terminal on the Mac that runs Claude Code: 
    $ curl -fsSL https://…/i/bcrc_enr_… | bash
  3. Scan the QR it prints with your phone — that pairing is the encryption root; the platform never sees the keys.
  4. Go. Your phone talks to your Mac through the relay: live sessions, terminals, permission prompts, session history.

Why it's safe

Blind relay

Traffic is sealed phone↔Mac with keys born from the QR pairing. The server routes opaque frames — no prompts, no code, no keys.

Your machine, your rules

Claude Code runs on your Mac exactly as it does today. Revoke a machine or a phone at any time from the dashboard.

No passwords stored

Magic-link sign-in; every credential is kept hashed. Free tier includes one Mac — Pro adds more machines and terminals.
